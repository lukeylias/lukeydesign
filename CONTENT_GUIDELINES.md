# Portfolio content guidelines

## Voice

- Write in a straightforward, restrained tone.
- Avoid whimsical language, inflated claims, and unnecessary self-promotion.
- Use "I" for direct ownership and "we" for team decisions and outcomes.
- Do not use em dashes.

## Paragraph length

- Keep body paragraphs to a maximum of four rendered lines in the default desktop web view.
- Split longer copy at a natural change in idea. Do not truncate or visually clamp the text.
- Give each paragraph one primary point and usually no more than two or three sentences.
- Check rendered line length in the browser after changing the copy.
- Allow text to wrap naturally on smaller screens, while keeping each paragraph focused and easy to scan.

## Case-study structure

- Make the sequence clear: problem, evidence, decisions, shipped experience, impact, and reflection.
- Connect important decisions to evidence and explain meaningful constraints or trade-offs.
- Distinguish between work that was proposed, tested, launched, and measured.
- Support outcome claims with a metric, timeframe, source, or specific qualitative finding.
