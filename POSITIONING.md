# Personal positioning

This document defines the core language used to describe Luke across the portfolio and external profiles. Adapt the wording to the space available rather than repeating the same sentence on every page.

## Core positioning

> I’m a Senior Product Designer focused on experimentation, AI and product strategy. I’m also hands-on with code, building tools that help me take ideas further.

This establishes the intended hierarchy:

1. Senior Product Designer is the primary identity.
2. Experimentation, AI and product strategy are the main areas of focus.
3. Working directly with code and building tools are differentiators that extend the design practice.
4. Accessibility, design systems, research and cross-platform delivery provide supporting evidence of range.

## Portfolio homepage

Use the core positioning beneath Luke's name:

> Senior Product Designer focused on experimentation, AI and product strategy. I’m also hands-on with code, building tools that help me take ideas further.

This replaces:

> Product design with technical depth. I design, write production code, and build AI deeply into my workflow.

The new version names the role and areas of focus first. It describes code and tools as part of how the work develops, without positioning Luke as a developer or using code as a credential.

## Homepage About teaser

Use a shorter supporting line that does not repeat the hero:

> My work spans experimentation, product strategy and delivery, with AI and code as part of the day-to-day process.

## About page opening

Use the core message in a more personal form:

> I’m Luke Ylias, a Senior Product Designer at nib. My work is focused on experimentation, AI and product strategy.
>
> I’m hands-on with code and build tools that help me explore and take ideas further. Accessibility and design systems are also consistent parts of my work.

Follow this with concrete examples from nib and Greater Bank. Let the work demonstrate principles and impact rather than adding a broad design manifesto.

## Short bio

For metadata, profile cards and other single-line contexts:

> Senior Product Designer focused on experimentation, AI and product strategy.

When there is room for a second sentence:

> Hands-on with code and building tools to take ideas further.

## External profile

For LinkedIn and similar profiles, use the full core positioning:

> I’m a Senior Product Designer focused on experimentation, AI and product strategy. I’m also hands-on with code, building tools that help me take ideas further.

## Supporting themes

Use these within project summaries, experience descriptions and case studies rather than forcing them into every headline:

- Accessibility capability and inclusive product delivery
- Design systems and multi-platform consistency
- Research, evidence and measurable experimentation
- End-to-end product ownership
- Prototyping, React and internal tool building

## Language to avoid

- Broad claims such as "outcomes over aesthetics" or "design should solve real problems."
- Describing the practice only as "technical depth" without naming what that means.
- Leading with accessibility when the broader product-design practice is the subject.
- Treating AI, code or tools as separate identities that compete with Senior Product Designer.
- Repeating the same positioning sentence across multiple sections of the same page.
